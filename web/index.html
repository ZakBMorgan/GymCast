    How to read this forecast

    Highlights mark the lowest occupancy among upcoming, fully scheduled-open hours. Partial and unknown hours are excluded. Closed hours stay in the data, and their predicted counts are unchanged. Occupancy predictions do not guarantee availability; scheduled hours may have unrecorded exceptions.
